⚡ Build from a template
Templates are structural blueprints — they specify movement patterns (squat, hinge, push…) and set/rep schemes, NOT specific exercises. When you generate a program from a template, MILO fills in actual exercises using your Coaching Principles and the athlete's Considerations. Mobility prep (ankle/hip/t-spine) is added automatically to every session.
