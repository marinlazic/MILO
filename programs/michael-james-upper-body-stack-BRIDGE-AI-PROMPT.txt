PROGRAM PARAMETERS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

· 4 upper-body sessions per week using a CLASSIC BODYBUILDING SPLIT:
    Day 1: Chest + Triceps
    Day 2: Back + Biceps
    Day 3: Shoulders
    Day 4: ARMS (bicep + tricep specialisation — the priority day)
· 40 minutes maximum per session
· Every session opens with 2 warm-up sets (~5 min)
· Working rep range: 8-12 for all hypertrophy work (NOT EMOM, NOT density,
  NOT dense-training, NOT "on the minute" — use straight sets, supersets,
  and drop sets ONLY)
· Athlete runs 2 self-managed lower-body sessions per week separately —
  DO NOT include any lower-body exercises in this program
· Use ONLY standard exercises that exist in Bridge Athletic's exercise
  library (each must have a video demonstration). NO custom moves, NO
  ViPR-style exercises, NO Institute of Motion drills, NO obscure
  variations. If an exercise isn't in the library, substitute it with
  the closest standard library equivalent.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
THE FOUR WORKOUTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DAY 1 · CHEST + TRICEPS (40 min)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WARM-UP (~5 min):
  · Push-Up × 10 reps (bodyweight)
  · Barbell Bench Press × 8 reps @ 50% Difficulty

WORKING SETS:
  1. Barbell Bench Press
     4 sets × 8 reps @ 75% Difficulty · Rest 90 sec

  2. Incline Dumbbell Bench Press
     3 sets × 10 reps @ 72% Difficulty · Rest 75 sec

  3. Cable Chest Fly
     3 sets × 12 reps @ 70% Difficulty · Rest 60 sec

  4. Triceps Rope Pushdown
     3 sets × 12 reps @ 70% Difficulty · Rest 60 sec

  5. EZ-Bar Skullcrusher
     3 sets × 10 reps @ 72% Difficulty · Rest 60 sec

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DAY 2 · BACK + BICEPS (40 min)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WARM-UP (~5 min):
  · Band Pull-Apart × 15 reps
  · Lat Pulldown × 8 reps @ 50% Difficulty

WORKING SETS:
  1. Pull-Up (sub Lat Pulldown if unable to do 8 bodyweight reps)
     4 sets × 8 reps @ 75% Difficulty · Rest 90 sec

  2. Barbell Bent-Over Row
     4 sets × 10 reps @ 75% Difficulty · Rest 75 sec

  3. Seated Cable Row
     3 sets × 12 reps @ 72% Difficulty · Rest 60 sec

  4. Dumbbell Bicep Curl
     3 sets × 10 reps @ 75% Difficulty · Rest 60 sec

  5. Hammer Curl
     3 sets × 12 reps @ 72% Difficulty · Rest 45 sec

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DAY 3 · SHOULDERS (40 min)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WARM-UP (~5 min):
  · Band Pull-Apart × 15 reps
  · Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press × 8 reps @ 50% Difficulty

WORKING SETS:
  1. Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press
     4 sets × 10 reps @ 75% Difficulty · Rest 90 sec

  2. Standing Barbell Overhead Press
     3 sets × 8 reps @ 78% Difficulty · Rest 75 sec

  3. Dumbbell Lateral Raise
     4 sets × 12 reps @ 70% Difficulty · Rest 60 sec

  4. Cable Face Pull
     3 sets × 15 reps @ 65% Difficulty · Rest 45 sec

  5. Bent-Over Dumbbell Rear Delt Fly
     3 sets × 12 reps @ 70% Difficulty · Rest 45 sec

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
DAY 4 · ARMS (40 min) — bigger-arms specialisation day
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WARM-UP (~5 min):
  · Dumbbell Bicep Curl × 10 reps light
  · Cable Triceps Pushdown × 12 reps light

WORKING SETS:
  1. EZ-Bar Bicep Curl
     4 sets × 10 reps @ 75% Difficulty · Rest 60 sec

  2. Close-Grip Bench Press
     4 sets × 10 reps @ 75% Difficulty · Rest 60 sec

  3. SUPERSET (back-to-back, no rest within):
     3a. Hammer Curl — 12 reps @ 72% Difficulty
     3b. Cable Triceps Pushdown — 12 reps @ 72% Difficulty
     3 supersets total · Rest 75 sec between supersets

  4. SUPERSET:
     4a. Preacher Curl — 10 reps @ 72% Difficulty
     4b. EZ-Bar Skullcrusher — 10 reps @ 72% Difficulty
     3 supersets total · Rest 75 sec between supersets

  5. Bicep Curl 21s (finisher)
     2 sets total · Rest 60 sec between sets
     (21s = 7 partial bottom-half reps + 7 partial top-half + 7 full ROM)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
12-WEEK PROGRESSION MAP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The four workouts above are the TEMPLATE. Loads and exercise variations evolve
across 12 weeks in three 4-week mesocycles:

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BLOCK 1 — FOUNDATION (Weeks 1-4) · Build the habit, dial in form
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

W1 (RE-ENTRY week — MJ has been off 6 weeks):
  · Drop primary load to 70% Difficulty (instead of 75% in template)
  · Drop secondary to 68%
  · Reduce sets by 1 on each exercise (4 → 3, 3 → 2)
  · Skip the 21s finisher on Day 4 this week
  · Goal: re-establish movement quality, no soreness

W2: Template volume + loads (75% on primaries, 72% on secondaries)

W3: +2.5% Difficulty on all primary lifts (so 77.5% on primaries)

W4 (DELOAD): Reduce all working sets by 1. All loads at 60% Difficulty.
  Skip drop-sets and 21s finisher. Use the week to recover.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BLOCK 2 — VOLUME BUMP (Weeks 5-8) · Add sets, introduce drop sets
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

W5: Add 1 set to every primary exercise (so 4 sets becomes 5).
  Loads: primary 75% · secondary 72%

W6: Same volume as W5. Add a DROP SET on the LAST exercise of every workout:
  · Day 1 (Chest+Tris): Drop set on final set of EZ-Bar Skullcrusher
  · Day 2 (Back+Bis): Drop set on final set of Hammer Curl
  · Day 3 (Shoulders): Drop set on final set of Rear Delt Fly
  · Day 4 (Arms): Drop set on final 21s set
  Drop-set protocol: after reaching failure, drop weight 30% and immediately
  rep to failure (no rest between).

W7: Same as W6 PLUS add a drop set on the second-to-last exercise of each
  workout. Loads: primary 78% · secondary 75%.

W8 (DELOAD): Back to W2 template volume, 60% Difficulty, no drop sets,
  no 21s.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
BLOCK 3 — INTENSIFICATION + EXERCISE VARIATION (Weeks 9-12)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

For Block 3, ROTATE primary exercises to introduce variety while keeping the
same set/rep schemes as Block 2 (5 sets primary, drop sets on last 2 exercises).

W9-W11 EXERCISE SWAPS:

  Day 1 (Chest + Triceps):
    · Swap Barbell Bench Press → Dumbbell Bench Press
    · Swap Cable Chest Fly → Dumbbell Chest Fly on flat bench
    · Swap Triceps Rope Pushdown → Overhead Cable Rope Triceps Extension

  Day 2 (Back + Biceps):
    · Swap Pull-Up → Weighted Pull-Up (or Lat Pulldown heavier)
    · Swap Seated Cable Row → Single-Arm Dumbbell Row
    · Swap Dumbbell Bicep Curl → Preacher Curl

  Day 3 (Shoulders):
    · Swap Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press → Arnold Press
    · Swap Dumbbell Lateral Raise → Cable Lateral Raise

  Day 4 (Arms):
    · Swap EZ-Bar Bicep Curl → Barbell Bicep Curl
    · Swap Close-Grip Bench Press → Dumbbell Skullcrusher
    · Swap supersets: replace Preacher Curl with Concentration Curl,
      replace EZ-Bar Skullcrusher with Cable Overhead Triceps Extension

LOAD PROGRESSION:
  W9: Primary 78% · Secondary 75% · 5 sets primary, 3 sets secondary
  W10: Primary 80% · Secondary 78% · same volume
  W11: Primary 82% · Secondary 80% · same volume
       (heaviest hypertrophy loads of the program)

W12 (BODY COMP RE-MEASUREMENT WEEK):
  This is NOT a 1RM strength test (this is a hypertrophy program).
  Measure: arm circumference (both biceps + triceps), chest circumference,
  body weight, progress photos. All four workouts run at W4 deload volume
  (3 sets primary, 2 sets secondary, 60% Difficulty, no drop sets, no 21s).

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
INTENSITY KEY (%Difficulty)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  82% = heavy hypertrophy / last 1-2 reps challenging
  78% = moderate-heavy working / last 2-3 reps challenging
  75% = typical hypertrophy working intensity (10 reps with 2 in tank)
  72% = moderate (12 reps comfortable, 13th would be a grind)
  70% = light working / drop-set bottom load
  65% = isolation / corrective work
  60% = deload week working intensity
  50% = warm-up set load
