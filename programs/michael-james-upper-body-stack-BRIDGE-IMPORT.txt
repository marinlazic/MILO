LOAD UNITS — IMPORTANT FOR BRIDGE IMPORT:
All loads in this program use RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion / "Difficulty %"),
NOT % of 1RM. When importing to Bridge AI builder, set the prescription mode to
"Difficulty %" or "RPE" so the athlete sees percent-of-effort guidance, not raw
% of 1RM. RPE values translate roughly: RPE 6 = 65%, RPE 7 = 75%, RPE 8 = 82%,
RPE 9 = 90%. Athlete picks the weight that matches the prescribed effort.

FORMAT KEY:
20D3 = 20 sets of 3 reps "on the minute" (Michael's density format)
10D5 = 10 sets of 5 reps on the minute
5D10 = 5 sets of 10 reps on the minute

WORKOUT A — DENSE BENCH STACK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Block 1: 20D3 Bench Press
 · 20 sets x 3 reps, on the minute
 · Load target: first set RPE 6 in W1, RPE 6.5 in W2, RPE 7 in W3
 · Pairs in 5 blocks of 4 sets:
   - Sets 1-4:   Standing Band Anti-Rotation Press, 6 reps each side
   - Sets 5-8:   KB Half-Kneeling Bottoms-Up Press, 5 reps each side
   - Sets 9-12:  Loaded Single-Leg Calf Raise, 12 reps each side
   - Sets 13-16: Band Pull-Apart, 12 reps
   - Sets 17-20: no pairing — focus on bench bar speed

Block 2: 10D5 Tempo DB Lateral Raise
 · 10 sets x 5 reps, on the minute, 3-second lowering tempo
 · Load target: RPE 7
 · Pairs in 2 blocks of 5 sets:
   - Sets 1-5:  Med Ball Rotational Slam, 4 reps each side
   - Sets 6-10: Tibialis Raise, 10 reps

Block 3: 5D10 Cable Triceps Pushdown
 · 5 sets x 10 reps, on the minute
 · Load target: RPE 7
 · All 5 sets pair with: Pallof Press hold, 15 seconds each side

Grip Finisher (Cardio):
 · Behind-the-back Barbell Wrist Curl, 3 sets of 20 reps, rest as needed

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WORKOUT B — DENSE PULL STACK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Block 1: 20D2 Chin-Up
 · 20 sets x 2 reps, on the minute
 · Load: bodyweight in W1-W2; add 2.5 kg in W3 if W2 felt sub-RPE 7
 · Pairs in 5 blocks of 4 sets:
   - Sets 1-4:   Ground-to-Stand drill with foam roller, 2 reps each leg
   - Sets 5-8:   Band Y's, 8 reps
   - Sets 9-12:  Wall T-Mob Rotational Reach, 4 reps each side
   - Sets 13-16: Loaded Double-Leg Calf Raise, 15 reps
   - Sets 17-20: no pairing — focus on chin-up bar speed and full ROM

Block 2: 10D5 Barbell Bent-Over Row
 · 10 sets x 5 reps, on the minute
 · Load target: RPE 7
 · Pairs in 2 blocks of 5 sets:
   - Sets 1-5:  Banded Rotational Wood Chop, 5 reps each side
   - Sets 6-10: Bumper Plate Pinch Hold, 15 seconds

Block 3: 5D10 Heavy DB Hammer Curl
 · 5 sets x 10 reps, on the minute
 · Load target: RPE 7-8
 · All 5 sets pair with: Glute Bridge, 12 reps

Grip Finisher (Cardio):
 · Bumper Plate Pinches, 4 sets of 15-20 seconds, rest as needed

WORKOUT C — DENSE OVERHEAD + INTEGRATED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Block 1: 20D3 Standing BB Overhead Press
 · 20 sets x 3 reps, on the minute
 · Load target: first set RPE 6 in W1, RPE 6.5 in W2, RPE 7 in W3
 · Pairs in 5 blocks of 4 sets:
   - Sets 1-4:   ViPR Shlift Lateral Step SS-Down, 3 reps each side
   - Sets 5-8:   ViPR Uppercut Back Step, 4 reps each side
   - Sets 9-12:  ViPR Trans Step with Up Shlift, 4 reps each side
   - Sets 13-16: Wall T-Mob Rotational Reach, 4 reps each side
   - Sets 17-20: no pairing — focus on full overhead lockout
 · If no ViPR (commercial gym), substitute:
   - KB Side-Step Press
   - Reverse Lunge with Single-Arm Press
   - KB Lateral Lunge with Pass-Through

Block 2: 10D5 Tempo Close-Grip Bench Press
 · 10 sets x 5 reps, on the minute, 3 sec down / 3 sec up
 · Load target: RPE 7
 · Pairs in 2 blocks of 5 sets:
   - Sets 1-5:  Tempo Close-Grip Pushup, 4 reps (3 down / 3 up)
   - Sets 6-10: Anti-Extension Plank Reach, 6 reps each side

Block 3: 5D10 EZ-Bar Skullcrusher
 · 5 sets x 10 reps, on the minute
 · Load target: RPE 7
 · All 5 sets pair with: KB Bottoms-Up Carry, 20 metres

Grip Finisher (Cardio):
 · DB or KB Farmers Carry, 4 sets of 25 metres, go heavy

WEEKLY PROGRESSION — PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD MAP
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Week 1 — Establish rhythm
 · All primary blocks (20D3 Bench, 20D2 Chin-Up, 20D3 OHP): first set RPE 6
 · All secondary blocks (10D5): RPE 7
 · All tertiary blocks (5D10): RPE 7
 · Goal: Find the load that lets you finish every set clean. Bar speed is the
   metric — if any rep slows, load is too high for next week.

Week 2 — Same volume, raise difficulty
 · Primary blocks: first set RPE 6.5 (small load nudge, ~2.5 kg on bench/OHP)
 · Secondary blocks: RPE 7.5
 · Tertiary blocks: same load, add 1 rep per set if previous week was clean
 · Goal: Same exercises, slightly harder. Pairing rhythm should feel locked in.

Week 3 — Heaviest dense week
 · Primary blocks: first set RPE 7 (another ~2.5-5 kg nudge)
 · Secondary blocks: RPE 8
 · Tertiary blocks: same load, add 1 more rep per set if W2 was clean
 · Goal: This is the test of the block. Last set should feel RPE 8.5-9.

Week 4 — DELOAD + RETEST
 · See separate test protocol below
 · No dense work. Find new 1RMs on the three core lifts.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
WEEK 4 — TEST WEEK
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Day A (Monday) — Deload Bench
 · Bench Press: 5 reps @ RPE 4, then 3 reps @ RPE 6, then 1 rep @ RPE 7. Stop.
 · No accessories. Walk away.

Day B (Wednesday) — Chin-Up & Row Test
 · Strict Chin-Up: max unbroken BW reps. Record: ____
 · Rest 5 minutes
 · BB Bent-Over Row: build to a 3RM. Record: ____ kg

Day C (Friday) — Bench + OHP Retest
 · Bench Press ramp:
   5 @ RPE 4 → 3 @ RPE 7 → 1 @ RPE 8 → 1 @ RPE 9 → attempt new 1RM
   Record new 1RM: ____ kg
 · Rest 5 minutes
 · Standing OHP ramp:
   5 @ RPE 4 → 3 @ RPE 7 → 1 @ RPE 9 → attempt new 1RM
   Record new 1RM: ____ kg

Compare to baseline. Expected delta: Bench +5-7 kg, OHP +2.5-5 kg,
Chin-Up +1-2 reps.
