▶ HOW TO IMPORT INTO BRIDGE — READ BEFORE PASTING ▼

  STEP 1: In Bridge, go to your program settings and set the prescription
          mode to "DIFFICULTY %" (sometimes labelled "RPE" or "Effort").
          DO NOT use "% of 1RM" — that mode will misread the loads below.

  STEP 2: Create a NEW program for Michael James → choose "PLAYLIST"
          structure (not scheduled / not weekly grid).

  STEP 3: For each of the 13 workouts below, create a workout in the
          playlist with that name and paste the corresponding block.

  STEP 4: After Bridge AI generates the first workout, spot-check that:
          - Each load reads "RPE X" or "X% Difficulty" (NOT "X% 1RM")
          - The "EMOM" and "Superset" structure is preserved
          - The grip finisher is in its own block at the end
          If any of those look wrong, redo step 1 and regenerate that
          workout. The first one is your canary — if it's clean, the
          rest will be too.

▶ SET-TYPE VOCABULARY USED BELOW ▼

  EMOM        Every Minute On the Minute — perform the prescribed reps in
              the first part of each minute, rest for the remainder.
  Superset    Two exercises performed back-to-back with no rest. Labelled
              "A1 / A2" (or "1a / 1b") inside the same block.
  Triset      Three exercises back-to-back (1a / 1b / 1c).
  Straight    Single exercise, all sets in one block, rest between sets.
  AMRAP       As Many Reps As Possible, single set, go to technical failure.
  Test set    Ramp single rep working to a true max.

▶ LOAD UNITS ▼

  All loads expressed as RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion):
    RPE 5 = could do 5+ more reps
    RPE 6 = could do 4 more reps
    RPE 7 = could do 3 more reps  ≈ "75% Difficulty"
    RPE 8 = could do 2 more reps  ≈ "82% Difficulty"
    RPE 9 = could do 1 more rep   ≈ "90% Difficulty"
    RPE 10 = max effort

WORKOUT 2: UPPER BODY STACK · A · DENSE BENCH (WEEK 1)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

BLOCK 1 · EMOM × SUPERSET · 20 rounds / 1 min per round
 1a. Bench Press: 3 reps @ first-set RPE 6
 1b. Pair (rotates every 4 rounds — see below)
 · Rest = whatever remains of each minute
 · Pair schedule:
     Rounds 1-4:   Standing Band Anti-Rotation Press, 6 reps each side
     Rounds 5-8:   KB Half-Kneeling Bottoms-Up Press, 5 reps each side
     Rounds 9-12:  Loaded Single-Leg Calf Raise, 12 reps each side
     Rounds 13-16: Band Pull-Apart, 12 reps
     Rounds 17-20: NO PAIR — focus on bench bar speed only

BLOCK 2 · EMOM × SUPERSET · 10 rounds / 1 min per round
 2a. Tempo DB Lateral Raise: 5 reps @ RPE 7 (3 sec lowering tempo)
 2b. Pair (rotates every 5 rounds):
     Rounds 1-5:  Med Ball Rotational Slam, 4 reps each side
     Rounds 6-10: Tibialis Raise, 10 reps

BLOCK 3 · EMOM × SUPERSET · 5 rounds / 1 min per round
 3a. Cable Triceps Pushdown: 10 reps @ RPE 7
 3b. Pallof Press Hold: 15 seconds each side

BLOCK 4 · STRAIGHT SETS · Grip Finisher
 · Behind-the-back Barbell Wrist Curl: 3 sets x 20 reps, rest as needed

NOTES FOR MJ (athlete-facing)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

· Pace yourself in the playlist. Aim for 3 upper-body sessions per week
  in A→B→C order. Your two lower-body sessions slot in between.
· No warmups are prescribed in this program — keep doing your tissue
  work and mobility on your own. First 2-3 sets of each EMOM are your
  specific warmup; ramp the load up to working RPE in those rounds.
· EMOM pairings are mandatory, not optional. They are the second half
  of each minute and what makes this dense work.
· If a rep slows down on the bench, drop 2.5-5 kg for the next set and
  hold that. Density is about quality reps under fatigue, not grinding.
· Calves, tibialis, glutes are woven into the EMOM pairs — that's your
  accessory lower body on these days. No extra LB needed here.
· Grip finishers at the end of every session. Keep them in.
