# Upper Body Stack — Michael James

**Block:** Upper Body Stack
**Athlete:** Michael James (48, 97 kg)
**Duration:** 4 weeks · 3 upper-body sessions per week (run alongside his 2 self-managed lower-body sessions)
**Source inspiration:** Benchuary 2026 (PPSA) — bench-press peaking block, re-cast in Michael's **dense (NDM)** format
**Built:** 2026-05-15 · Coach: Marin Lazic

---

## Why this block

Michael's *ARM FARM MJ* block ran out at the end of March — six weeks of detraining on the upper body. Bench, overhead, and pull patterns are due a fresh push. This block uses **Benchuary 2026's bench-heavy progression** as the engine, but recast into Michael's preferred density-on-the-minute format with paired complementary work, no warmups (he runs his own mobility), and accessory lower-body work (calves, tibs, glutes) slotted into the dense blocks so he can keep his two dedicated lower-body sessions clean.

Three drivers across the block: **measurable progression** on bench / chin-up / overhead, **variety per session** for the upper body, and **integrated core + power** woven through every block via band rotations, ViPR-style work, and rotational throws.

## SESSION A — DENSE BENCH

**1) 20D3 Bench Press @ 67% Bench 1RM**
*20 sets of 3 reps, on the minute, paired in 5 blocks of 4*

- **Sets 1-4** ⇢ pair: **Standing Band Anti-Rotation Press** — 6 reps / side · *integrated core*
- **Sets 5-8** ⇢ pair: **KB Half-Kneeling Bottoms-Up Press** — 5 reps / side · *integrated shoulder + grip*
- **Sets 9-12** ⇢ pair: **Loaded Single-Leg Calf Raise** — 12 reps / side · *accessory LB*
- **Sets 13-16** ⇢ pair: **Band Pull-Apart** — 12 reps · *posterior shoulder*
- **Sets 17-20** ⇢ no pairing — focus on bench quality + bar speed

**2) 10D5 Tempo DB Lateral Raise · 3 sec lower**
*10 sets of 5 reps, on the minute, paired in 2 blocks of 5*

- **Sets 1-5** ⇢ pair: **Med Ball Rotational Slam** — 4 reps / side · *power core*
- **Sets 6-10** ⇢ pair: **Tibialis Raise** — 10 reps · *accessory LB*

**3) 5D10 Cable Triceps Pushdown**
*5 sets of 10 reps, on the minute*

- All 5 sets ⇢ pair: **Pallof Press Hold** — 15 sec / side · *anti-rotation*

**Grip Finisher · "Cardio"**
Behind-the-back BB wrist curl — 3 sets of 20, rest as needed.

## Weekly progression

| Week | Bench (Session A) | OHP (Session C) | Chin-Up (Session B) | Theme |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **W1** | 20D3 @ **67%** | 20D3 @ **65%** | 20D2 BW | Establish rhythm. Bar speed is the metric — if any rep slows, the load is too high for next week. |
| **W2** | 20D3 @ **70%** | 20D3 @ **67.5%** | 20D2 BW · perfect tempo | Volume identical, load nudged. Pairs stay the same exercises. |
| **W3** | 20D3 @ **72.5%** | 20D3 @ **70%** | 20D2 + **2.5 kg** | Heaviest dense week. Last week before test. |
| **W4** | **TEST week** — see below | **TEST week** | **TEST week** | Deload first half, retest second half |

Secondary blocks (10D5 lateral raise, BB row, close-grip bench) follow the same +2.5-5% drift across W1→W3. Tertiary blocks (5D10 pushdown, hammer curl, skullcrusher) progress on **reps to a number Michael chooses** rather than load — he picks a weight that lets him finish 5×10 clean in W1 and adds 1 rep per set per week if it stayed clean.

## Notes for Michael

- **No warmup is prescribed** — keep doing what you're doing (tissue work / mobility / juggling). The first set of each dense block is your specific warm-up; lower the load on the first 2-3 sets if you need a longer ramp into the working weight.
- **Pairings are mandatory** — they're not "if you have time", they're the second half of the minute. They're what keeps this dense and what makes it work as integrated training.
- **If a rep slows down** on the dense bench (or any primary), drop the load 2.5-5 kg for the next set and don't let bar speed degrade further. Density is about quality reps under fatigue, not grinding.
- **Accessory lower body** (calf raises, tibialis, glute bridges) is woven in — you don't need to do extra. Heavy lower-body work happens on your two self-managed sessions.
- **Grip finishers** at the end of each session — keep them, they're the difference between strong wrists and a tweaky elbow.
- **Power / integrated core** every session: band rotations, ViPR work, med ball, KB bottoms-up — these are the "power" thread you asked for.
